# Fishroom

Welcome to **Fishroom** — your digital fishroom, minus the water spills.

Fishroom is an app built for aquarium hobbyists who want to **log tank data, track progress, and flex their aquascapes** with the community.

---

## 🌊 What is Fishroom?

Fishroom helps you keep tabs on your aquariums while connecting with other fish nerds (said lovingly).

Whether you’re running a single nano tank or a full-blown fish empire, Fishroom lets you:

* Log tank entries (parameters, notes, observations)
* Track changes over time
* Share photos of your tanks
* Engage with a community of fellow hobbyists

Basically: fewer sticky notes, more fish pics.

---

## ✨ Features

### 📓 Tank Logs

* Record water parameters, maintenance, and notes
* Build a historical timeline for each tank
* Spot trends before things go… algae-green

### 📸 Photo Sharing

* Upload and share tank photos
* Show off progress shots and aquascapes
* Inspire (or humble) other hobbyists

### 🌍 Community

* Discover tanks from other users
* Share insights, setups, and ideas
* Learn from real-world hobbyist experiences

---

## 🐟 Who is this for?

* Freshwater or saltwater hobbyists
* Beginners learning the ropes
* Veterans who track *everything*
* Anyone who’s ever said “just one more tank”

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. Create an account

3. Start logging entries
4. Post photos
5. Vibe with the community

No gatekeeping. No judgment. Only fish.
